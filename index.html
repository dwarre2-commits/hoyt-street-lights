Since 2016 / Our journey

🎄 How It All Began

From a small holiday display to a synchronized light show.

Hoyt Street Lights began in 2016 with just one lighting controller and six inflatables. What started as a small Christmas display has grown year after year, with new props, lights, music and improvements along the way. Today, our display features approximately 20,000 RGB, CCR and LED lights powered by 10 controllers. We look forward to continuing the tradition and making each season even more magical!
